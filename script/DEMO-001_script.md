## A. The part
L01: This is a cartridge lock. A fictional one, built for this demo.
L02: Its whole job is to hold a cartridge in place, with one movement anyone can learn.

## B. Lug and groove [short: S01 "Why it only goes in one way"]
L03: The cartridge carries a single lug on its side.
L04: Line it up with the open groove in the housing, and the lug guides it straight down.
L05: There is only one way in, so it cannot go in crooked.

## C. The quarter turn [short: S02 "A quarter turn, then one click"]
L06: At the bottom of the groove, a quarter turn carries the lug sideways.
L07: At the end of the turn, a spring lifts it into a notch, behind the amber stop.
L08: You hear it as one click.

## D. The section [short: S03 "Why pulling does nothing"]
L09: Cut the housing in half, and you can see why it holds.
L10: The spring keeps pushing the cartridge up, so the lug stays pressed against the stop.
L11: Pull on it, and nothing moves. To release it, you push down, and turn back.

## E. Close
L12: One lug. One groove. One quarter turn.
